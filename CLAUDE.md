## Engineering invariants

1. Commit decoded rows/cursor atomically before the verbatim8-byte HISTORY_END ACK;never ACK
   partial data or regenerate a trim token. ACK can permanently remove band flash.
2. decoded_onehz replaces on(device_id,ts_ms);evict its RR beats in the same batch. No promise
   of raw replay after bytes are no longer retained.
3. Missing/thin/invalid input stays null,Metric.absent or an honest estimate/reason;never measured zero.
4. Analytics output needs reviewed kAlgoVersion/changelog;rows are immutable per version.
   Pure renames need no bump. Package-output changes include real source/version decisions.
5. Track both package trees/path lock;no floating refs or pubspec_overrides.yaml. Review digests.
6. Local labels use todayLabel/dayLabelOf;epochs remain absolute. No86400-second day assumption.
   Keep effective,eaten,recorded and logged provenance distinct.
7. One substrate.dart decode,sleep/readiness/ingest path and NotificationCenter.emit emitter.
8. Never prune raw/decoded before full successful derivation;partial scalars do not finalize a
   day. raw_archive is not a complete backup. Heavy analytics stays off UI;re-arm worker ambient state.
9. Migrations are additive,idempotent,cheap,sequential with same-version repair. WAL uses rawQuery;
   never move a live SQLite main file without WAL state.
10. HeadlessSyncGate.tryRun skips,never queues. Export/coach readers respect allow-listed views.
11. High-rate live data stays RAM-only;phone-only steps and HR-only live-band scope remain.
12. Never auto-send dangerous trim/reboot/power-cycle/firmware opcodes.
13. Reset stays inside CalculationStore.resetWith through native/preferences completion and
    capture/derive shutdown;no re-entry or restoring an anchor after wipe.

Check finally/timeout latch release,late-result ownership,idempotence,notification dedupe,
post-commit revisions and failure/retry/cancellation for schema/media/backup mutations.
Synthetic assertions/channels/pages certify no physical radio,sensor or OS delivery.
